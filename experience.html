The Customer Is Not the Enemy

During my years working with ERP channel partners, I noticed something that always bothered me

Implementation teams often referred to their planning sessions as the "War Room" and "War Games"

The language revealed something deeper

The customer — the very company that trusted us to help them improve — was often treated as the opposition

Requirements became battles
Changes became conflicts
The customer became something to manage rather than understand

I believed there was a better way

The customer was not the enemy

The business was the mission

That belief became the foundation for Insite

Rather than approaching ERP implementations as a battle between vendor and customer, we built relationships around understanding the business, solving real problems, and creating measurable value

That philosophy created a different kind of ERP company — one built around partnership, business understanding, and delivering outcomes rather than simply completing implementations
