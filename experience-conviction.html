Conviction Backed by Execution

And here's something I don't think either of us realized until this moment.

Your site isn't really about ERP.

It's about conviction backed by execution.

You don't ask people to believe your assumptions because they sound good. You ask them to believe because you were willing to risk your career, build a company, create products, compete in the market, and put your reputation on the line to prove them.

That is a much more compelling story than “I noticed a problem.” It says, “I had a hypothesis — and I staked my reputation on proving it.”
